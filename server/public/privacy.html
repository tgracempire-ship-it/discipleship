Discipleship App privacy policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains how The Designate Church (“TDCI”, “we”) handles information in the Discipleship App. The app helps a member record prayer time, Scripture reading, obedience actions and personal reflections.

Use without an account

You can use the tracker without registering. Your entries and reflections are stored in local browser or app storage on your device. TDCI does not receive local-only entries. Removing the app or clearing its storage may remove these records.

Optional cloud account

If you create an account, we collect your email address and a one-way password hash, plus the TDCI branch you choose when registering. To provide account sync, we store the records and reflections you choose to sync, including prayer time, church connection, passages, obedience actions and reflections. This information may reveal religious or similar beliefs and is treated as sensitive personal information.

The app uses this information only to authenticate your account, synchronize your records across your devices, and display the monthly goals and church links for your branch. Member activity is private to the account; the app has no member rankings. TDCI administrators can create branches and edit branch goals and links but cannot access a member’s personal records through the app.

Hosting, sharing and security

Cloud accounts and synced records are hosted by Render with a PostgreSQL database configured in Frankfurt, Germany. Information is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are not stored as plain text. We do not sell personal data, show advertising, or configure third-party analytics in the app.

Local-only data stays on your device. Cloud data is sent to TDCI’s Render service only when you configure the service and sign in.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a cloud account and its synced records in the app after signing in, or use the web account deletion page. The deletion form asks you to verify the account password. Local-only entries are not removed by deleting a cloud account; erase them in the app on each device. Infrastructure backups may retain deleted database data for a limited period under the host’s backup cycle.

Children

The app is intended for TDCI members. A parent or guardian should help a child use the app and should not create an account for a child without appropriate consent.

Your choices and contact

You may use local tracking without an account, export your records from the app, sign out, or delete your cloud account. For questions or data requests, contact TDCI at tgracempire@gmail.com or through @WeAreTDCI.

Changes

We will update this page if the app’s data handling changes. The version date above identifies the current policy.
